Intent Scorer for Tripworks

Tour operators get hundreds of leads, and reps dial them in whatever order the CRM lists them. I designed and built a feature that watches how shoppers behave on an operator's booking site, turns it into a 0–10 intent score, and pushes the hottest leads to the top of the CRM so reps know who to call right now.

The problem

Most CRMs list leads by date or alphabetically. Reps work down the list, so their best hours go to people who signed up for a newsletter, while the shopper who picked a date, typed their email and then closed the tab waits until tomorrow. By then they've booked with a competitor.

Tours are impulsive, time-boxed purchases ("what are we doing Saturday?"). Speed-to-lead matters more here than almost anywhere, and checkout abandoners are the easiest revenue to recover. Someone just has to call them first.

The idea, in three moments

🌐

Capture behaviour

A snippet on the operator's site records visits, experience clicks, pages viewed, booking clicks and abandoned checkouts.

🔥

Score it transparently

Five signals roll up into a 0–10 score with fire tiers, from Cold to Call Now. Every point can be explained.

📞

Put it where reps work

A native Intent column sorts the CRM by default. Leads scoring 8+ get a pulsing "Call now" badge and a one-line reason to call.

How the score works

No black box. Each signal is capped so no single behaviour can dominate, then weighted by how strongly it predicts a booking. Reps can open any lead and see which behaviours earned which points.

Booking clicks3.0
Abandoned checkout2.5
Return visits1.5
Experience clicks1.5
Pages researched1.5
score = Σ min(value ÷ cap, 1) × weight × 10
Cold < 5 🔥 Warm 🔥🔥 Interested 🔥🔥🔥 Hot 🔥🔥🔥🔥 Very Hot 🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥 Call Now

In the demo, Alex arrives with three earlier visits at 4.8 (Cold). They compare tours, click Check Availability and Book Now, type their email and leave. That's 9.2 (Call Now), and they're #1 in the CRM a few seconds later.

Design decisions

Native, not bolted on. The Intent column, "Hot Leads Right Now" KPI and Intent filter reuse the CRM's own cards, pills and spacing, so the feature reads as part of the product on day one.

Explain before you ask for trust. Clicking a score opens a drawer with a bar per signal, the full website timeline, and a plain-English summary: "Viewed Kayaking 9 times across 4 visits, clicked Book Now, and abandoned checkout just now." Reps only follow a ranking they believe.

A demo that tells the story without me. The prototype is built so someone watching for 60 seconds with no narration understands the feature: a live signal tracker, captions, and a single moment where the lead visibly climbs to the top.

What it should move

Lead-to-booking conversionThe primary outcome
Time-to-first-contactFor leads scoring 8+
Revenue per rep hourSame team, better order

The plan is to A/B test it across rep cohorts: intent-sorted ordering against the default ordering, with scores logged silently in the control arm so we can check calibration. The next version would learn the weights from historical bookings and decay older sessions over time.
